# About uione

uione is made by [Daher Alfawares](https://www.linkedin.com/in/daheralfawares).

It's one idea built three ways at once: a feature should be said once, plainly, and
everything else should follow from it. The [mission](/mission) says why.

## What's here

- **A language and its compiler.** A feature's data, rules, commands, views,
  permissions and screens are written in one short `.one` file. The compiler, `one`,
  turns it into plain React, Go, Firestore rules and Pulumi: code you can read, keep
  and change. The [language](/language) has every part of it.
- **A studio.** The studio, here at uione.io, puts the whole language in a browser:
  it checks every line as you type, shows what each one becomes, commits to your
  GitHub repository, and deploys to your own Google Cloud project. There's nothing
  to install.
- **Software built with it.** [neotrac](https://neotrac.org) is open project
  tracking, written entirely in uione: projects, boards, issues with their whole
  history, a roadmap and a wiki. It's a thank-you to Trac, and the proof that the
  language holds up in a real product.

## Open

The compiler is free software under the AGPL, and the libraries apps are built on are
under the LGPL, so an app of any kind can use them. The source is on
[GitHub](https://github.com/da0x/uione), and every release is listed on
[releases](/releases).
