# 0.11.1

Released 2026-10-13. The compiler and every library move to 0.11.1 together. A
record named from its title is renamed with it, and everything that points at it
follows; uione.io has a page showing one screen in all three apps; and the desktop
apps keep a title's room beside its buttons.

## New

- **A key made from a field follows it.** A record keyed by `slug(title)` that's
  given a new title moves to the id the new title makes, in the same save, and an
  update form can ask for the key itself. What points at it points at the new id,
  what's keyed by it moves too, as a board's stages move with the board, its
  history goes with it, and numbers counted per record, like a board's issues, go on
  from where they were. The old title is free again, and the old address still
  opens it in the web, desktop and terminal apps; on the web the address bar
  changes to the new one. Roles and services keep the names they were made with,
  since a defined role is found by its name and a service is known by its id.
- **Targets, on uione.io,** at [/language/targets](/language/targets): neotrac's
  board as it's written and as the web, desktop and terminal apps draw it, what
  each target writes and draws, and how to build and run each.

## Fixes

- **The desktop apps' buttons beside a title.** A button that opens a form took the
  whole row among them, so a board's buttons stacked down over its title. They
  stay on one line beside it.
- **Desktop and terminal apps built against an older uione.** A build folder kept
  the libraries an earlier configure found; `UIONE` in the environment now wins on
  every configure.
- **The error for an element a target can't draw** suggests the targets that can as
  `--target` takes them, `--target web,desktop`.
- **A site serving an image** has a source for every line of it, and pictures on
  docs pages are as wide as the text at most.
