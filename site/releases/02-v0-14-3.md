# 0.14.3

Released 2026-10-14. The compiler and every library move to 0.14.3 together. A
list switched between a table, a board, a pipeline and a whiteboard has one track
above the switch, its milestones green or red as their cards went, and a board
chimes only for a card that arrives.

| Part | Where | Install |
|---|---|---|
| `one`, the compiler, for Linux and macOS | [GitHub releases](https://github.com/da0x/uione/releases/tag/v0.14.3) | one command, see [Install](/install) |
| The libraries desktop and terminal apps are built on | [GitHub releases](https://github.com/da0x/uione/releases/tag/v0.14.3) | installed with the compiler, in `~/.local/share/uione/0.14.3` |

## Fixes

- **A track over a list switched between ways of showing it** is one track, the
  first any of them says, drawn once above the switch rather than inside the table
  and the board, each in its own way, and not at all over the pipeline and the
  whiteboard. Whichever is shown, it looks the same, and each keeps to its pick,
  on the web, the desktop and in the terminal.
- **A milestone where any card failed** has a red dot on its track, beside the
  green of one where every card succeeded. The table's track shows them too, worked
  out from the stages of the board beside it.
- **A board chimed for cards a pick showed**: picking a milestone on its track, or
  searching, brought cards already in a column that chimes into view, and each
  chimed as if it had just arrived. Only a card that does arrive chimes now.
