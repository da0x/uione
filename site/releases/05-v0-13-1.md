# 0.13.1

Released 2026-10-14. The compiler and every library move to 0.13.1 together. A
pipeline shows a list's cards a lane for each kind, as a release's packages, websites
and apps, each along its own stages; and a canvas's regions make it a whiteboard with
columns, a card let go in another moving there.

## New

- **Pipelines.** `pipeline board_page.issues by stage over board_page.stages
  { lanes by labels  path by kinds  status by status  title }` puts each card in a
  lane for its kind, as its labels say, and along its row the stages its kind goes
  through, as each stage's `kinds` lists: those before where it is ticked, the one
  it's at in its status's color, the one it failed in red, as its data's `at` says,
  and those to come faint, with what its holder last said at the row's end. In the
  web, desktop and terminal apps, as the classic deploy screen drew one.
- **Whiteboards with columns.** In a canvas, `regions by width` draws a band down it
  for each of its `over` rows, side by side, as wide as the row says, its title along
  its top. `move issue::move along board_page.steps` moves a card let go in another
  band there, as a board's card is moved, and `resize stage::update` saves a band
  made wider or narrower by dragging the line after it, with a column-resizing
  cursor on the desktop, or with `<` and `>` in the terminal.
