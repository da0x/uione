# 0.17.0

Released 2026-10-23. The compiler and every library move to 0.17.0 together.
Apps that run as a business: subscriptions sold through Stripe, everyone limited in
how fast they may ask and how much they may make, a list of everyone who has signed
up with what each uses and pays, and a project built on another that adds to it
without changing it. Organizations keep an audit of what's done in them, suspend
members, and give roles to whole teams, and a project can change its address.

### New

- **Subscriptions through Stripe.** `webhook stripe` keeps a record of each
  customer, and a command can say `stripe checkout`, `portal`, `change` and
  `cancel`. A Stripe address can name the record's fields, like
  `/organizations/{slug}/billing`.
- **Every app is protected from abuse.** Each person and service is limited in how
  fast they may ask and how much of each kind they may make in a day, 1000 unless
  `at most` says otherwise, and told how long to wait. The clients wait that long
  and ask again. A person turned off in Firebase is refused within a minute.
- **A project can extend another.** It's built on the other's files and adds to
  them: `extend role` adds commands to a role the other defines, and
  `extend command` adds requires to the other's commands, which can count and look
  for records in any namespace.
- **A list of everyone.** `each user` in a view lists everyone who has signed up, a
  page at a time, for a role given `user::view`: what each has run, written and
  keeps, sums like what they paid, and on a person's own page, lists of what's
  theirs.
- **Audit.** Every command in a place, and in what it holds, is recorded with it,
  read by the roles a view names, downloaded from a table as CSV, and gone once
  older than kept.
- **Suspended members** are set aside with everything they held, holding nothing
  until restored, and gone for good after 90 days.
- **Team grants.** A record can give a role in a place to everyone in a group, given
  and taken as they join and leave.
- **`keep at least 1 owner`** refuses removing the last one, so a place is never
  left without the role it keeps.
- **A command can change a key typed as it is**, like a project's address, with
  `changes slug`. The record moves with everything named by it, in batches in the
  background, and its old address leads to the new one.
- **Search by words.** A searchable field finds records by the words in it.
- **A field pointing at another namespace's entity** is picked from a person's own
  list of them by name, and a link in words can go to an address with a view's
  values in it.
- **A project with no backend** deploys as its web app alone, on Hosting, with no
  billing.

### Fixes

- **A form showing a stage's name renames it by its title**, since a key sent back
  as it was doesn't hold it.
- **Files attached to a record that moves are kept**, where they were deleted with
  its old address.
- **A card shows its holder's progress only while they hold it.**
- **A page keeps its heading** when what it shows reads like the app's name.
- **A view's C++ method is named by its namespace too**, when another namespace has
  a view of the same name.
