# 0.6.13

Released 2026-10-07. The compiler and every library move to 0.6.13 together. A list
in a view now says `order by` and a table `sort by`, and the checker offers to fix
each that doesn't; nothing else that worked in 0.6.12 changes. `one upgrade` records
the new version.

## New

- **Steps between phases.** A command like `issue::move` takes the field its
  `changes` names. `was issue.phase` is what a field held before the command, and
  `exists(step where from == was issue.phase && to == issue.phase && held(roles))`
  asks whether there's a step the person's roles may take, so who moves what where
  is the project's to say. `issue::move along project_page.steps` is a button for
  each step from where an issue is now that the person may take.
- **Picking another entity.** A form field that points at another entity, like a
  step's phases, picks from a list of them a view on the screen holds; a list of
  them, like a step's roles, is ticked.
- **Order and sort by.** `order by created_at descending` in a view's list and
  `sort by number descending` in a table.
- **A row opens wherever it's clicked**, as its first cell's link does; its own
  buttons still do their own thing.

## Fixed

- A person's picture stays a circle when corners are square.
- PaperColor's text is a shade darker, so a page of it reads easily.

## For a component set of your own

`Steps` draws a workflow's step buttons. A table's row that has a `link` may open it
from anywhere on the row.
