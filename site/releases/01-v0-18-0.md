# 0.18.0

Released 2026-10-23. The compiler and every library move to 0.18.0 together.
Front pages say more, and a sixth design draws them: what a site offers in groups,
each with its icon and what it holds, the one sentence it stands for, the steps it
works in, and links that write an email or call. Each is said in a few plain lines,
and every design draws them its own way.

### New

- **constellation, a sixth front page design.** The site's name in two tones over a
  network of points drawn toward the pointer at night, and a blueprint grid by day;
  a band of everything it offers; cards that rise into view; its statement lit word
  by word as it's scrolled past; its steps along a line that fills; and what it asks
  in a panel with a turning edge. In the theme's colors, and still for anyone who
  asks for less motion.
- **What a front page offers, in groups.** A section of sections is a group, its
  title over them, and each can hold its own, like services each with their parts.
- **`overline "What we do"`** is the short line over a hero's or a section's title.
- **`icon database`** draws one of twelve new icons beside a section's title:
  `building`, `chart`, `cloud`, `code`, `connect`, `database`, `globe`, `layers`,
  `lock`, `people`, `shield` and `spark`. Buttons and links can use them too.
- **`statement "..."`** is the one sentence a site stands for, set large.
- **`section "How we work" as steps`** numbers its sections, done in order.
- **Email and phone links**, `link "mailto:hello@example.com" "Write to us"` and
  `link "tel:+15555550100" "Call us"`.
- **Every design draws all of it**, and so do the desktop and terminal apps.

### Fixes

- **An upgrade records the version in the project's own block**, not in the block
  of a project it extends.
- **The compiler is built from components**, each compiled once, so it builds
  faster, and a release publishes the compiler the checks built and tested.
