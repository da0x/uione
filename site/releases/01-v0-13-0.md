# 0.13.0

Released 2026-10-14. The compiler and every library move to 0.13.0 together. A
canvas shows a list's cards placed anywhere, dragged and resized, in the web,
desktop and terminal apps; a list is shown while a condition holds, so one screen
shows the same cards as a board or a canvas; cards tag their choices; and a choice
added with a default reaches what was stored before it.

## New

- **Canvases.** `canvas board_page.issues by stage over board_page.stages
  { place issue::place  grid 160 from 40 80  title }` shows a list's rows as cards
  placed where their `x`, `y`, `w` and `h` say, on a surface that pans and zooms.
  A card is dragged to move it, or by its corner to resize it, and once let go it's
  placed by the command, snapped to the grid, shown there at once and back if the
  command turns it down. A card not placed yet starts in its stage's column, as a
  board shows it. `kind kind` draws a card as a note or as large text.
  - On the web it's declarative SVG, drawn by `Surface` from `@uione/react`, which
    any kit draws its cards on; the wheel pans, Ctrl and the wheel zoom, and on a
    phone one finger pans and two pinch.
  - The desktop and terminal toolkits each have a `surface` for any app: the
    desktop's dragged and resized with the pointer, the terminal's boxes moved a
    grid step at a time with the arrows, Shift resizing them.
- **A list shown while a condition holds.** `when` after a table's, board's, cards'
  or canvas's view and link, like `when board_page.style == style::canvas`, so one
  screen shows the same cards as a board or a canvas, and switching keeps every card
  where it was in both. A table and a board shown while the same condition holds are
  still one, switched between.
- **Cards tag their choices.** A choice a card shows, like a board's visibility, is a
  tag beside its title: Public, Private. `hide when empty` leaves cards out while
  there are none, or while the reader can't read them.
- **A C++ client sends any command by its name** with `said("projects::service::key",
  json)`, and reads its whole answer, like a service's new key.

## Fixes

- **A choice added with a default** after records were stored left them with none,
  which no view or rule reads as anything; a board made before boards could be
  private went missing from its project's page. The backend now gives each its
  default once, when it starts, rebuilding the views that show it.
