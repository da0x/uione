# 0.14.1

Released 2026-10-14. The compiler and every library move to 0.14.1 together. A
backend whose `once` moves records off a choice there's no longer starts again, and
a record's history keeps what was changed, not who was holding it.

## Fixes

- **A `once` that dispatches an update to the record its `each` picked**, like
  `dispatch stage::update { status = status::success }` for a stage whose status was
  done, which is no longer a choice, was refused for the status it was moving the
  stage off, and the backend didn't start. The record `each` hands its body is now
  checked only when the body changes it.
- **A record's history** no longer keeps who held it, till when, and what they
  reported: an agent taking a card that waits, and letting it go, again and again,
  filled its timeline. Its card shows them live. What was kept of them before is
  cleared from each history once, when the backend starts.
- **Updating a record with a `data` field** no longer keeps a change to it from
  nothing to nothing in its history.
- **Named values in a timeline**, like a card's data, show by name, `status
  working, runner 2`, rather than as `[object Object]` on the web.
